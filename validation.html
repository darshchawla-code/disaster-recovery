How accurate is AidAtlas?

Every earthquake since 2001 that killed 250 or more people (NOAA NCEI catalogue) is replayed through the same forecast the map uses, with no knowledge of what happened. The chart shows the forecast range for deaths beside the toll that was reported. A good model has about 8 in 10 reported tolls inside its 10–90 % range.

Summary

Not run on this device yet. A run reads towns for each event from OpenStreetMap (about 2–4 minutes); results are kept on this device.
