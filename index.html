Forecast the need. Fair-share the aid. Route it fast.

AidAtlas predicts where a disaster will hit hardest, how many people will need water, food, shelter, medical care and responders, and moves stock from the nearest supply stores to them at the lowest feasible cost. Allocation follows need, not head-count or proximity, and it re-optimises every six hours as roads close, supplies land and the disaster spreads.
